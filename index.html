Ways to work with BrightPlay.

Relationships

Theraplay®-informed practice

Deepen your connection. Built on the bond you already have, our play sessions combine lighthearted, nurturing fun with gentle structure and just-right challenges. Through carefully led activities, we foster emotional trust, co-regulation, and that unmistakable feeling of being in sync.

Pacific Beach studio sessions

Interplay

PROJECT ImPACT

Small moments, big leaps. From breakfast to bath time, your family’s rhythm sets the tempo. Through hands-on parent coaching, we weave practical strategies into the routines you know by heart, inviting more back-and-forth, copycat antics, and giggles every day.

In-home appointments

Expression

Functional communication

Your child has plenty to say. From picture exchange to speech-generating devices, there is more than one way to get a message across. With personalized guidance and purposeful practice, we open up opportunities to express preferences, make choices, share ideas, and experience the power of being understood.

Studio, home, or school settings

Belonging

Group programs

In good company. Knowing how to join in is one thing; feeling that you belong is another. From Theraplay®-informed group games to UCLA PEERS® for Preschoolers, small, lively groups help children find common ground, navigate differences, and discover the pleasures of friendship. Parallel parent sessions carry new skills well beyond the group.

getting started

From hello to play.

No need to have it all figured out. That’s what our time together is for.

  1. 1

    First, a conversation

    A relaxed, 15-minute call to get acquainted, share what’s on your mind, and see whether BrightPlay feels right for your family.

  2. 2

    Next, playful discovery

    In the studio or at home, a few joyful activities reveal what makes your child light up. Afterward, we decide, side by side, what matters most.

  3. 3

    Then, expert guidance

    We bring structure, materials, and savoir-faire; you’re free to simply be yourselves. Ongoing feedback and reflection shape each session around you.
