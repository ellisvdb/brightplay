Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date of publication]

BrightPlay™ is a parent-child coaching practice in Pacific Beach, San Diego, owned and operated by Ellis van den Boogaard as a California sole proprietorship. In this policy, “BrightPlay,” “we,” and “us” mean that practice. Families trust us with their children, their homes, and their time. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who may see it, and how we protect it.

Our commitment to discretion

  • We don’t confirm to anyone that your family works with us, unless you ask us to or the law requires or allows it, for example a court order, a subpoena, or a child-safety investigation.
  • We schedule studio sessions so families don’t overlap in the waiting area.
  • We never post client stories or case examples.
  • We never use photos or videos of your child in marketing unless you give separate written permission. Saying no has no effect on your services.

Information we collect

Through this website

  • Details you choose to send us by email or through our booking page, such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred times, and your message.
  • Standard server logs kept by our website host, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. Our host uses these to keep the site secure and running.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking tools on this website.

When you become a client

  • Contact details for parents or guardians, your child’s name and date of birth, and emergency contacts.
  • The people you authorize to attend sessions, receive updates, or pick up your child.
  • Intake information: your family’s priorities, your child’s interests and ways of communicating, and any health, allergy, or accessibility information that helps us plan safe sessions. For example, we ask about food allergies because some activities include a small snack.
  • Session notes, goals, and progress notes, and observation materials, including Marschak Interaction Method (MIM) materials where we use them.
  • Video recordings, made only with your separate written consent (see Recordings below).
  • Custody orders or parenting plans, where they affect who makes decisions for your child.
  • Billing and payment records. Our payment processor handles card details; we don’t store full card numbers.
  • For services funded through a regional center program, the information needed to authorize and pay for those services, such as plan objectives and attendance records.

We collect information about your child from you, as a parent or legal guardian. With your written permission, we may also receive information from your child’s school, other providers, or regional center.

How we use information

We use personal information only to:

  • respond to inquiries and schedule introductions;
  • plan, provide, and review coaching services;
  • assess your child’s and your family’s progress, and review that progress with you;
  • take part in professional supervision and training, only as you authorize in writing;
  • communicate with you and keep accurate service and billing records;
  • process payments and work with funding sources you choose;
  • protect the safety of children, families, and our practice; and
  • meet our legal obligations.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Who may receive information

We share information only as needed, and only with:

  • you, and the people you authorize in writing, such as a co-parent, nanny, grandparent, or house manager;
  • your family’s Theraplay® supervisor, a Certified Theraplay® Supervisor, and, if a session is submitted for certification review, reviewers at The Theraplay Institute; only with your separate written consent, which names them;
  • service providers that help us run the practice, such as our encrypted email and calendar provider, scheduling service, payment processor, and website host, limited to what each needs to do its job;
  • for funded services, your Financial Management Services provider or regional center service coordinator, as needed for approved services and payment; and
  • government agencies, courts, or others when the law requires or allows it, including child abuse reporting, court orders, and subpoenas.

Records of individuals served through a regional center

When we provide services to a person with a developmental disability through a regional center program, we keep those records confidential under California Welfare and Institutions Code section 4514, and we release them only as that law allows.

Recordings

  • Theraplay® Informed Practice sessions are video recorded for professional supervision. We record only the session itself, never arrivals, waiting time, or conversations in the reception area.
  • For Project ImPACT coaching, we may record a short play sample at the start and later in coaching, with your consent, so you can see progress for yourself.
  • Before we record anything, you sign a separate consent that explains the purpose, names who may view the recording, and describes how we store it.
  • We use recordings only for assessment, for your family’s own review, and for Ellis’s supervision and professional training. We never post them publicly, on social media, or in advertising.
  • If you prefer no recording, we’ll suggest a service that works well without it.
  • We keep recordings, like all service records, for at least five years from the date of final payment for the services, or until any audit findings are resolved, whichever is longer. Then we securely delete them. Until then, they stay encrypted and locked away.

How we protect information

We keep records on encrypted devices and encrypted drives, stored in a locked safe, and we don’t keep them in general cloud storage. Our email and calendar run through an encrypted provider. Only Ellis has access, plus the people you authorize as described above. No system is perfectly secure, and email and text messages carry some risk. If you prefer another way to share sensitive information, tell us and we’ll arrange it.

How long we keep records

  • Service records, including recordings, session notes, attendance, and billing: at least five years from the date of final payment for the services, or until any audit findings are resolved, whichever is longer.
  • Agreements, consents, and incident notes: until your child’s 21st birthday, or five years from the date of final payment, whichever is later.
  • Inquiries from families who don’t enroll: one year after our last contact.

At the end of each period, we securely delete or shred the records, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices and requests

Parents and legal guardians may ask to see the information we hold about them or their child, request a copy or a correction, update communication preferences, or withdraw an optional consent for the future. Send requests to hello@brightplay.org. We verify identity and legal authority before releasing a child’s records, and we respond within 30 days. The law requires us to keep some records for a set time, so we may not be able to delete them right away.

Do Not Track and third-party tracking

This website doesn’t track visitors over time or across other websites, so it doesn’t respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals; there is nothing to switch off. We don’t allow other companies to track your activity across websites through our site. When you book through our scheduling page, our scheduling service processes the details you enter under its own privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

This website is for parents, guardians, and professionals. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

If a security incident happens

If an incident affects personal information, we investigate promptly and notify affected families as California law requires.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a meaningful way, we post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and we email current clients.
