Approach

Approach

Every family arrives with its own rhythm. We plan each session around your child’s interests and your family’s priorities, and you take part in every step. Sessions take place in our Pacific Beach studio, in your home, or, for some services, at school or in community settings.

Theraplay® Informed Practice

Relationships

Pacific Beach studio sessions

Deepen your connection. Built on the bond you already have, our play sessions combine lighthearted, nurturing fun with gentle structure and just-right challenges. Through carefully led activities, we foster emotional trust, co-regulation, and that unmistakable feeling of being in sync.

Four ingredients, thoughtfully combined: comfort and care, shared delight, gentle structure, and just-right challenges.

Ellis is completing the Theraplay® certification practicum under a Certified Theraplay® Supervisor. Sessions are video recorded for supervision; our Privacy Policy explains how we protect every recording.

Suited for

Any family that wants more ease and closeness, especially when big feelings, transitions, or new chapters (a sibling, a move, a new school) make connection harder to find.

How it unfolds

  1. A relaxed introductory conversation.
  2. A parent consultation, adults only, about your family’s story and hopes.
  3. A playful observation in the studio (the Marschak Interaction Method): you and your child try a few simple activities together.
  4. Feedback and planning: we look at the highlights together and choose a focus.
  5. Regular parent-child sessions, typically starting with about 16 weeks, with parent check-ins along the way.
  6. A shared review of how far you’ve come.

PROJECT ImPACT

Interplay

In-home appointments

Small moments, big leaps. From breakfast to bath time, your family’s rhythm sets the tempo. Through hands-on parent coaching, we weave practical strategies into the routines you know by heart, inviting more back-and-forth, copycat antics, and giggles every day.

Five practical steps, woven into everyday moments: focus on your child, adjust your communication, create opportunities, teach new skills, and shape the interaction.

Project ImPACT is an evidence-based, parent-mediated program developed by Brooke Ingersoll, PhD, and Anna Dvortcsak, MS, CCC-SLP. Ellis completed introductory and advanced training with Anna Dvortcsak and has delivered Project ImPACT coaching with families through a regional center provider.

Suited for

Children up to age six whose social and communication skills are still unfolding, with or without a diagnosis.

How it unfolds

  1. A relaxed introductory conversation.
  2. A home visit with a ten-minute play sample, recorded with your permission, so you can later see how far you’ve come together.
  3. Goal setting, side by side: you choose what matters most.
  4. Coaching inside your own routines: 60-minute sessions, once or twice a week, over 12 to 24 weeks.
  5. A fresh play sample and a review together.

Functional communication

Expression

Studio, home, or school settings

Your child has plenty to say. From picture exchange to speech-generating devices, there is more than one way to get a message across. With personalized guidance and purposeful practice, we open up opportunities to express preferences, make choices, share ideas, and experience the power of being understood.

Our coaching supports, and never replaces, assessment or device recommendations by a licensed speech-language pathologist. Augmentative and alternative communication meets the evidence-based practice criteria of the National Clearinghouse on Autism Evidence and Practice (2020).

Suited for

Children who have plenty to say and are still finding the way that works best for them, whether they use a few words, many, or none yet.

How it unfolds

  1. A relaxed introductory conversation.
  2. Discovery, in the studio, at home, or at school: how your child already communicates, and what they most want to say.
  3. Choosing tools together, such as picture exchange, picture boards, or a speech-generating device, working alongside your child’s speech-language pathologist or school team whenever applicable.
  4. Practice across the day, including with teachers and caregivers, so the message lands everywhere.

Group programs

Belonging

School and community settings · Coming soon

In good company. Knowing how to join in is one thing; feeling that you belong is another. From Theraplay®-informed group games to UCLA PEERS® for Preschoolers, small, lively groups help children find common ground, navigate differences, and discover the pleasures of friendship.

Suited for

Preschool, kindergarten, and elementary-age children who are ready to practice the art of friendship.

How it will work

  • Theraplay®-informed group games, in schools and community settings, led by one or more expert guides.
  • UCLA PEERS® for Preschoolers, for ages four to six, with parallel parent sessions.

Interested in a group? Write to belong@brightplay.org, and we’ll let you know when groups open.

Observations, not diagnosis

Play-based observations, including the Marschak Interaction Method (MIM) where it fits, shape our coaching goals and session plans. They aren’t a medical, psychological, or developmental diagnosis, and they don’t replace a clinical evaluation. For concerns about your child’s health or development, please consult your pediatrician. We’re happy to help you find the right community resources.

From hello to play

  1. First, a conversation. A relaxed, 15-minute call to get acquainted, share what’s on your mind, and see whether BrightPlay feels right for your family.
  2. Next, playful discovery. In the studio or at home, a few joyful activities reveal what makes your child light up. Afterward, we decide, side by side, what matters most. Before coaching begins, we go over goals, fees, privacy, and recording together, and you sign our services agreement.
  3. Then, expert guidance. We bring structure, materials, and savoir-faire; you’re free to simply be yourselves. Ongoing feedback and reflection shape each session around you.

Every child grows at their own pace, so we can’t promise a particular result. We can promise careful preparation, full attention, and honest, ongoing feedback.
