Funding & Resources

Private pay

Most families invest in BrightPlay™ coaching privately. We share fees, packages, and cancellation terms in writing before you enroll. We don’t bill health insurance.

California’s Self-Determination Program

Families in the Self-Determination Program choose the services and supports that best fit their needs and plan objectives. Under the program, service providers don’t need to be vendored by a regional center; only the Financial Management Services (FMS) provider does.

If your family takes part in the program, you may be able to include an eligible BrightPlay service in your spending plan, paid through your FMS provider. Eligibility, approval, and payment depend on your approved spending plan, provider qualifications, and program rules. We’re happy to provide what your FMS needs to verify our qualifications, such as our business registration, insurance certificate, and background check.
