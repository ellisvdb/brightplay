Accessibility

Effective date: [date of publication]

Our studio

The building at 1940 Garnet Ave is wheelchair accessible, with a ramp at the front entrance and an elevator to Suite 220. Parking is taken care of: BrightPlay has its own reserved space in the building’s garage, and designated accessible spaces are there too, so you won’t need to search for a spot. We send the details when you book.

If you need an accommodation for a session, tell us when you book. For some services, sessions at home are also an option.

Our website

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. If any part of the site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, such as large print or plain text, contact us. We respond within five business days and provide the information in a format that works for you.
